AtticLab · CHUDOKU

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

01 · Overview

CHUDOKU (“the App”) is developed and operated by AtticLab. The App does not require sign-up. When you first open it, an anonymous account is created automatically so your score can appear on the daily ranking.

02 · Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account ID — created automatically. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Nickname and character — the nickname and ghost character you choose.
  • Daily score — your total points for the day, used for the daily ranking.
  • Daily Challenge time — how long you took to clear the Daily Challenge, used to show your percentile.

Settings, game progress (level, items, streaks) and statistics (play time, rounds played) are stored only on your device.

03 · How We Use Information

  • To run the daily ranking and show your rank.
  • To show your Daily Challenge percentile.

04 · What Other Players Can See

Your nickname, character, daily score and rank are shown to other players in your daily ranking group. Please do not use your real name in your nickname.

05 · Service Providers

  • Supabase — stores your anonymous account, nickname, character, daily score and Daily Challenge time (servers in Seoul, South Korea). Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the App. AdMob may collect device information, IP address and, if you allow tracking, your advertising identifier to serve, personalize and measure ads. You can change ad consent in Settings → Privacy Choices (where required) and tracking permission in your device settings. Privacy policy

06 · Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your data while you use the App. To delete your account, nickname, character, scores and Daily Challenge records, use Settings → Delete My Data, or email us at the address below.

Deleting the App removes the data stored on your device, but not the data on our server. Use Delete My Data for that.

07 · Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will delete it.

08 · Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@atticlab.app.

09 · Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
