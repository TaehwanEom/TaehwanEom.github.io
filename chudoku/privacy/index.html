Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

01 · Overview

CHUDOKU (“the App”) is developed and operated by AtticLab. The App does not require sign-up and does not create an account.

02 · Information Stored on Your Device

  • Settings — music, sound and vibration on/off.
  • Profile — the nickname and character you choose.
  • Game progress — your level, the round in progress, items, win streak, daily points and daily streak stamps.
  • Statistics — play time, rounds played, levels cleared and candies found.

All of this is stored only on your device. The current version of the App does not send it to us or to anyone else.

03 · Sharing

If you use Share, your device’s share sheet opens with a link to this website. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

04 · Ads and Online Features

The current version of the App has no ads and no online ranking. If we add them in a future update, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe what is collected and by whom.

05 · Deleting Your Data

Deleting the App removes all of the data above from your device.

06 · Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

07 · Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@atticlab.app.

08 · Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
