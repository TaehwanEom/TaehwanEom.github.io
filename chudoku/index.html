CHUDOKU

A candy logic puzzle. Find the hidden candies before the ghost takes your lives.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it plays

One candy each

Every row, every column and every color area hides exactly one candy. Candies never touch each other.

Mark with X

Tap to mark cells that can’t be a candy. Double-tap where you think a candy is hiding.

Three lives

A wrong guess costs a life. Clear the board with lives left to win the level.

Helpful items

Use Candy, Hint and X items when you get stuck.

Win streaks

Clear levels in a row to raise your multiplier up to x5. Your remaining lives become today’s points.

Daily streak

Come back every day to stamp your calendar and collect items.
