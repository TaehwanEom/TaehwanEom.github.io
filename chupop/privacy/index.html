AtticLab · CHEW POP

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

01 · Overview

CHEW POP ("the App") is developed and operated by AtticLab. The App does not require sign-up. When you first open it, an anonymous account is created automatically so your scores can appear on the global ranking.

02 · Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account ID — created automatically. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Nickname — generated automatically. You can change it in Settings.
  • Game data — your scores, best score, and round records (the round's random seed and your moves), which we use to verify that scores are genuine. We also record the app version.
  • Achievement progress — totals such as rounds played, combos and special candies used, so your achievements are kept if you reinstall the App.
  • Profile photo (optional) — only if you choose one. It is resized to 256×256 before upload.
  • Photo reports — if you report another player's photo.
  • App integrity key — Apple's App Attest is used to confirm that requests come from the genuine App on a real device. It does not identify you.

Settings and your local best score are stored only on your device. Achievement progress is stored on your device and on our server.

03 · How We Use Information

  • To run the global ranking and show your rank.
  • To verify scores and prevent cheating.
  • To display and review profile photos, and to act on reports.

04 · What Other Players Can See

Your nickname, profile photo (if you set one), best score and rank are shown to other players on the global ranking. Please do not use your real name or a photo of yourself or anyone else in your nickname or profile photo.

05 · Service Providers

  • Supabase — stores ranking data and profile photos (servers in Seoul, South Korea). Privacy policy
  • OpenAI — profile photos are sent to OpenAI's moderation service for an automatic content check before they are saved. Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the App. The App requests non-personalized ads only. AdMob may collect device information and IP address to serve and measure ads. If you watch a rewarded ad to continue a round, AdMob sends us a confirmation that includes your anonymous account ID and the round ID, so we can verify the reward. Privacy policy

06 · Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your data while you use the App. You can delete your profile photo at any time in Settings. To delete your account, nickname, scores, achievement progress and photo, use Settings → Delete My Data, or email us at the address below.

Deleting the App removes the data stored on your device, but not the data on our server. Use Delete My Data for that.

07 · Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided a photo or other information, please contact us and we will delete it.

08 · Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@atticlab.app.

09 · Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
