How it plays

Place blocks

Drag the blocks onto the board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.

Splash themes

Keep clearing to move on to the next theme — fruits, slime, candy, ice, porcelain and more.

Daily ranking

Your best score of the day goes on today’s ranking. See where you stand and try again.
