AtticLab · ChuBlock Splash

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

01 · Overview

ChuBlock Splash (“the App”) is developed and operated by AtticLab. The App does not require sign-up. When you first open it, an anonymous account is created automatically so your score can appear on the daily ranking.

02 · Information We Collect

  • Anonymous account ID — created automatically. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Nickname and avatar — the nickname and avatar you choose.
  • Daily score — your best score of the day, used for the daily ranking.

Settings, your best score and the game in progress are stored on your device. Your settings, nickname and avatar are also kept in your device’s secure storage (Keychain) so they remain if you reinstall the App on the same device.

03 · How We Use Information

  • To run the daily ranking and show your rank.

04 · What Other Players Can See

Your nickname, avatar, daily score and rank are shown to other players in your daily ranking group. Please do not use your real name in your nickname.

05 · Service Providers

  • Supabase — stores your anonymous account, nickname, avatar and daily score (servers in Seoul, South Korea). Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in the App. AdMob may collect device information, IP address and, if you allow tracking, your advertising identifier to serve, personalize and measure ads. You can change tracking permission in your device settings. Privacy policy

06 · Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your data while you use the App. To delete your account, nickname, avatar and scores, use Settings → Delete My Data, or email us at the address below.

Deleting the App removes most data stored on your device, but not the data on our server or in your device’s Keychain. Use Delete My Data for that.

07 · Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will delete it.

08 · Contact

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@atticlab.app.

09 · Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
